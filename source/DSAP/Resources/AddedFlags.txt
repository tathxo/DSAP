For added shop item lots.
To try to avoid collisions as much as possible, I went as unused as possible:
digit 1 = 6, mostly unused but valid span of flags. Some map objects / ObjActs seem to change flags in this range.
digit 2-4 = 132, Ash Lake/Great Hollow..which is mostly empty of interactable objects
digit 5-8 = offset to 2000, up to 3999. Past 5000 would reset.
Shop item counts need 8 or 10 flags per slot, but I stick with 10 because the game does.
